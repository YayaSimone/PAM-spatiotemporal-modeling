# Research Questions and Validation Plan

**Independent Computational Biology Research | 2026–Present**

**Researcher:** Kenya S. Montagueo

## 1. Research Objective

This project explores theoretical host–pathogen interactions
and neuroinflammatory injury within a mathematical framework
motivated by primary amebic meningoencephalitis (PAM).

The current Phase I implementation is nonspatial, empirically
uncalibrated, and experimentally unvalidated.

## 2. Primary Research Question

What mathematical insights and limitations emerge from
representing interacting biological processes within a
coupled theoretical framework?

The original scientific hypothesis is preserved in the
[Original Working Framework](original-framework.md).

## 3. Current Research Scope

The current phase focuses on:

- Mathematical consistency.
- Numerical verification.
- Documentation of modeling assumptions.
- Computational reproducibility.
- Interpretation of theoretical model behavior.
- Identification of scientific limitations.

The current formulation is described in the
[Mathematical Framework](mathematical-framework.md).

## 4. Future Research Directions

The original conceptual framework proposes spatial modeling
and comparison with relevant biological observations.

These directions remain prospective and are not presented
as completed research.

## 5. Validation Status

The current project has not undergone experimental validation,
clinical validation, or independent peer review.

Numerical verification should not be interpreted as evidence
of biological accuracy or clinical predictive capability.

## 6. Research Limitations

- Model parameters remain empirically uncalibrated.
- Operational biological units remain unresolved.
- The spatial framework remains proposed.
- Independent computational reproduction remains outstanding.
- No validated clinical or therapeutic conclusions are claimed.
