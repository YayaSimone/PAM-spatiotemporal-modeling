# Scientific References

## Purpose

This bibliography documents the scientific and computational literature informing the theoretical research framework, along with background literature for future research development.

Published evidence is distinguished from original modeling assumptions and computational observations.

## Clinical and Biological Literature

### Chen and Moseman (2023)

Chen, C.-w., & Moseman, E. A. (2023). Pro-inflammatory cytokine responses to *Naegleria fowleri* infection.

*Frontiers in Tropical Diseases*, 3, 1082334.

DOI: [10.3389/fitd.2022.1082334](https://doi.org/10.3389/fitd.2022.1082334)

Provides biological background on host immune responses and inflammatory signaling.

### Gharpure et al. (2021)

Gharpure, R., Bliton, J., Goodman, A., Ali, I. K. M., Yoder, J., & Cope, J. R. (2021). Epidemiology and clinical characteristics of primary amebic meningoencephalitis caused by *Naegleria fowleri*: A global review.

*Clinical Infectious Diseases*, 73(1), e19–e27.

DOI: [10.1093/cid/ciaa520](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/ciaa520)

Provides clinical and epidemiological background on primary amebic meningoencephalitis (PAM).

## Mathematical and Dynamical Systems Literature

### di Bernardo et al. (2008)

di Bernardo, M., Budd, C. J., Champneys, A. R., & Kowalczyk, P. (2008). *Piecewise-smooth dynamical systems: Theory and applications.*

Applied Mathematical Sciences, Vol. 163. Springer.

DOI: [10.1007/978-1-84628-708-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-84628-708-4)

Provides background literature on nonlinear dynamical systems and mathematical terminology.

### Murray (2003)

Murray, J. D. (2003). *Mathematical biology II: Spatial models and biomedical applications* (3rd ed.).

Interdisciplinary Applied Mathematics, Vol. 18. Springer.

ISBN: 978-0-387-95228-4

Included as background reading for proposed future mathematical research. Spatial modeling has not been implemented in the current framework.

## Sensitivity and Identifiability Literature

*Bioinformatics*, 25(15), 1923–1929.

DOI: [10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358)

## Evidence Boundaries

- Published literature does not independently validate the proposed mathematical framework.
- Model parameters remain hypothetical and empirically uncalibrated.
- Computational observations are not experimental findings.
- Numerical verification does not establish biological validity.
- Background literature does not imply that all referenced methodologies have been implemented.
- Additional literature review and experimental validation remain outstanding.
