## Research Overview

This independent research project explores mathematical
modeling and scientific computing in the context of
primary amebic meningoencephalitis (PAM).

The current phase focuses on theoretical mathematical
formulation, computational documentation, numerical
consistency, and reproducibility.

The framework is hypothetical, empirically uncalibrated,
and experimentally unvalidated.

## Research Objectives

- Develop experience in mathematical biology.
- Apply scientific computing to theoretical biological systems.
- Examine mathematical assumptions and numerical consistency.
- Establish transparent research documentation.
